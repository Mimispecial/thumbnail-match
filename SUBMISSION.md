Title: Thumbnail Match

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable thumbnail campaign with separate semantic screening and audience choice. The owner freezes a creative brief, visual anchors, and audience segments; independent designers declare layouts; GenLayer validators return an anchor mask plus CLEAR, CROWDED, or MISMATCH. Only clear designs enter voting, each address votes once per segment, and deterministic non-tied tallies select segment winners. No pixel inspection or click prediction is claimed.
